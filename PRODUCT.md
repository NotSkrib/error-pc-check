# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Error SMP owners, admins, and checkers use the staff console while coordinating consensual screenshare checks for Minecraft Java players. Owners and admins manage staff; checkers issue one-time session keys, monitor scans, and review reports.

## Product Purpose

The console coordinates a transparent, consent-based forensic check and presents its evidence for human review. Success means staff can issue the correct one-time access details, understand session progress, and review findings without confusing evidence, coverage gaps, or severity with an automated verdict.

## Brand Personality

Clear, measured, accountable. The interface should feel trustworthy and practical under live moderation work, never accusatory or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid surveillance dashboards, punitive or gamified language, interfaces that imply guilt from a severity score, and decorative effects that compete with forensic evidence.

## Design Principles

- Make consent and the scope of each check easy to understand.
- Present findings as evidence for a person to review, never as an automated punishment decision.
- Keep live-session status, case identity, and next actions easy to scan.
- Minimise visual noise so technical details remain legible.
- Treat retention and access boundaries as part of responsible operation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard focus visible, use clear labels and status text in addition to color, support reduced motion and reduced transparency, and keep the workflow usable on narrow screens.
